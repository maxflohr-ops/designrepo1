# Bounty Sounds Privacy Policy (DRAFT — needs counsel review before publishing)

_Last updated: [DATE]. Host at https://bountysounds.com/privacy._

Bounty Sounds ("we") runs a marketplace where artists fund bounties on TikTok
sounds and clippers are paid for verified views. This policy covers the
Bounty Sounds iOS app and API.

## What we collect

- **TikTok account data, with your consent:** your open id, display name,
  follower count, and — for videos you submit to a bounty — video ids and
  view counts, read through TikTok's official APIs after you authorize us via
  TikTok login. We never see your TikTok password.
- **Bounty activity:** claims, submissions, checklists, disputes, appeals,
  and the ledger of amounts you've earned or funded.
- **Payout and payment data:** handled by Stripe. We store only Stripe's
  account references — never card numbers or bank credentials. Stripe's
  privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy.
- **Device data:** a push token if you enable notifications, and device
  attestation results (Apple App Attest) when you cash out.

## What we use it for

Running the board (matching, ranking, notifications), counting views to
calculate payouts, fraud prevention (spike detection, duplicate checks,
account-age floors), dispute resolution (we keep every view sample so a
disputed count can be re-derived), tax reporting where required (via Stripe),
and legal compliance.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't run third-party ads or ad tracking. We
don't host your videos — they stay on TikTok.

## Sharing

Stripe (payments/payouts/KYC), Apple (push notifications), TikTok (the APIs
you authorized), and service providers under contract (hosting, error
logging). We disclose if legally required.

## Retention

Ledger entries and view samples are append-only financial records and are
retained as long as legally required. Account data is deleted on request
except where money-movement records must be kept.

Disconnect TikTok, delete your account, or export your data by emailing
[SUPPORT EMAIL]. Notification permissions are controlled in iOS Settings.
